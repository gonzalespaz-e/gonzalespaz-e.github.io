Events

The Ward One Community brings former residents, families, and supporters together through gatherings that honor the neighborhood’s history and lasting connections.

Members have participated in Columbia’s King Day at the Dome celebration and have also come together for reunions, banquets, and group trips. These occasions keep the spirit of Ward One present across generations.
